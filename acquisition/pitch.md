# Governance SaaS — Constitutional Engine

**Problem:** Most SaaS is a black box. Buyers cannot see why decisions were made, where failures occur, or how to audit them.

**Solution:** A governance SaaS with constitutional authority → validation → decision → evidence → verification → replay → audit baked in. Every decision has replayable justification and a failure surface map.

**Differentiation**
- Evidence-first decision chain
- Replayable lineage via CAR/CDR/CEL/CPE
- Failure Surface Map shows where/why system fails
- AMUL architecture: Adaptive, Modular, Universal, Logical
- Aligned with CIEMS sovereignty stack

**Core Modules**
Governance Engine, Decision Lineage, Failure Surface Map, Audit & Replay, Governance Console, Acquisition Package

**Demo Flow**
Intent Submission → Constitutional Chain → Evidence Buffers → Decision Lineage Replay → Failure Surface → Audit Ledger

**Pricing**
$15k–$50k for governed systems; $50k–$250k+ with MRR

**Buyers:** Compliance, risk, regulated SaaS, AI governance teams.

This is infrastructure, not a toy SaaS.
